The idea

Four daysOne mountain

Big Sky Resort in January — deep terrain, wide open bowls, and no crowds worth mentioning. We land in Bozeman, drive into the Gallatin Canyon, and spend three nights in the mountains with a full kitchen, a fireplace, and nowhere else to be. Here's everything you need to know.

The mountain

Big Sky Resort

"The Biggest Skiing in America" isn't just marketing — four interconnected zones (Mountain Village, Moonlight Basin, Spanish Peaks, and the Lone Peak summit) add up to some of the most varied terrain in the country. We're skiing two of our three full days on a general lift pass, so we've got one day built in to rest, explore, or take a lap at your own pace.

The outlook

Watching the sky

Southern Montana doesn't have a strong seasonal signal either way this year — historically it runs close to average, with the better odds for a big snow year showing up in March. Nothing to worry about now; we'll watch the 10-day forecast as the trip gets closer.
